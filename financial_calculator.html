Method & sources

Historical decision dates, one-year horizon. Forecasts use Ridge for ZHVI and Elastic Net for ZORI, both fitted to one-year growth. Model settings were selected with 2015–2019 training origins and 2021 validation origins; the final models were fitted to those two samples together. Saved test forecasts cover 2023–2024. New 2025-origin forecasts reuse that same frozen fit, with no fitting on test outcomes or on unavailable 2026 outcomes. All ten 2025 predictors are present; 2025Q4 county labor and inflation inputs use only November–December. These are retrospective data snapshots, not an as-of-release backtest. Model selection remains tentative because the eligibility rule was finalized after viewing test results. These estimates are not guarantees.

Historical mode uses observed home-value and rent growth with the same assumed costs and investment return; it is not an observed household outcome. Housing levels and historical growth retain the supplied project's data. County-wide ZHVI and ZORI do not necessarily represent equivalent homes. Decision quarters are historical snapshots, not exact transaction dates.

A 30-year fixed mortgage uses the quarter's national rate. Savings use an assumed constant annual effective return, initially the quarter's one-year Treasury yield as a proxy. Monthly savings are invested at month end. Property tax, insurance and maintenance use the initial price. The model assumes sufficient income for the monthly budget; it does not assess mortgage qualification. Excludes income/capital-gains taxes, tax deductions, HOA dues, utilities, moving costs and renter insurance. Minimum down payment is an illustrative 3%, not a lending eligibility rule.
